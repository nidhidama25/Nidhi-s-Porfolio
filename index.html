Most analysts start with data. I started with people complaining. Working in B2B SaaS support, I kept hearing the same problems — not because the product was broken, but because nobody was asking why users got stuck in the first place. So I started writing SQL queries. Not because anyone asked me to. Because the patterns were too loud to ignore.

// chapter 01 — how i got here
2023

the support desk.

Data & Support Analyst at Udyog Software. B2B SaaS ERP environment. I was closing tickets — but also reading the data behind them. Why did 40% of onboarding queries come from the same three screens? Nobody had asked that question before me.

2024

the first queries.

Taught myself SQL. Built my first retention analysis — not for a course, for a real question I couldn't stop thinking about. Started thinking in cohorts, funnels, activation loops. Realized I wasn't a support person who liked data. I was an analyst who happened to start in support.

2025

the investigations.

Building the kind of analysis I wish existed. Studying why D2C brands fail on Instagram. Modeling why restaurants churn on Swiggy. Learning Python. Building dashboards. Turning questions into case studies that actually explain something — not just display metrics.
